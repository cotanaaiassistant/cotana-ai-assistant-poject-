Frequently asked questions

Cotana hears my voice but does not react. What can I do?

Check MIC ON, your selected microphone, and try typing “cotana open youtube” first. Check recognized text in the app log. Voice recognition may hear “kodana” instead of “cotana”.

Why do I get a python313.dll error?

Run the EXE from dist\FriendCotanaV17, not build\FriendCotanaV17. Keep the _internal folder beside the EXE.

Does Cotana need the internet?

Some speech recognition and voice features use online services. Ollama local AI can run on your computer after model setup.

Can I turn off automatic startup?

Yes. Use Cotana's startup setting, or Windows Settings → Apps → Startup.

Why is the download button unavailable?

The public installer link has not yet been added. The website intentionally avoids sending you to a non-existent file.

How do I uninstall Cotana?

Open Windows Settings → Apps → Installed apps, find Friend Cotana, and choose Uninstall. For a portable build, remove the app folder and its startup entry if enabled.

Privacy & release notes

Cotana uses microphone input when voice listening is enabled. Online speech recognition and text-to-speech services may process audio or text. Ollama responses run locally when configured.
